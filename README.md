# Power-BI-Business-Insights-Sales-View-Dashboar

Short Discription :

The Sales View is a Power BI dashboard focused on analyzing sales performance across customers, products, regions, and markets. It provides an interactive view of revenue, gross margin, and sales drivers to support business decision-making.

Key Metrics

Net Sales – Revenue generated during the selected period.

Gross Margin $ – Gross profit after relevant costs.

Gross Margin % – Gross margin as a percentage of net sales.

Regional Performance – Comparison of sales and margin across regions and countries.

Dashboard Features

Interactive Year and Quarter filters

Filters for Region, Market, Customer, and Segment/Category

Customer Performance table with Net Sales, Gross Margin $, and Gross Margin %

Product Performance analysis by product segment

Performance Metrics scatter chart comparing Net Sales and Gross Margin

Country-level sales and margin comparison

P&L Value by Description visualizations

Unit Economics analysis

Interactive filtering across visuals

Main Analysis Areas

Customer Performance

Shows customer-level Net Sales, Gross Margin, and Gross Margin %, helping identify high-value and profitable customers.

Product Performance

Breaks down performance by segments such as Desktop, Notebook, Accessories, Peripherals, Networking, and Storage.

Regional & Country Performance

Compares Net Sales and Gross Margin across countries and regions to identify strong and weak markets.

P&L & Unit Economics

Visualizes major P&L components and their contribution to overall financial performance, supporting deeper sales and profitability analysis.

Business Questions

The dashboard helps answer:

Which customers generate the highest sales?

Which product segments are most profitable?

Which countries or regions perform best?

Where are high sales supported by healthy margins?

What factors are driving sales performance?
